### Key Industry Catalysts
1. **The Shift from Chat to Workflows:** Over 70% of enterprise AI spend is moving from single-turn chatbots to multi-turn agentic workflows (software engineering, customer operations, legal document extraction, financial auditing).
2. **The "Model Explosion":** AI labs release new model variants (Pro, Flash, Flash-Lite, Thinking, Distilled) every few weeks. Engineering teams cannot keep up with manual benchmarking.
3. **The FinOps AI Mandate:** CFOs and Engineering VPs are demanding proof of ROI and aggressive cost reduction without compromising task completion rates.

## 🚀 Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

1. **Phase 1: Viral Distribution (Months 1–3):**
   * Launch the Public Leaderboard during the Google Cloud All Things Agentic Hackathon.
   * Publish weekly *"State of Agentic Economics"* reports on X, LinkedIn, and Hacker News comparing new model releases (e.g., Gemini 3.5 Flash vs. Claude 3.5 Haiku on real SWE-Bench tasks).
2. **Phase 2: Router & Harness Partnerships (Months 4–6):**
   * Provide free API access to open-source routing frameworks (Not Diamond, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen).
   * Launch official VS Code / Cursor plugins displaying the Benchpress "Why Switch?" rationale overlay.
3. **Phase 3: Enterprise FinOps Expansion (Months 7–12):**
   * Sell enterprise VPC-deployed benchmark harnesses to Fortune 500 engineering teams to systematically test internal agent pipelines before deploying to production.
